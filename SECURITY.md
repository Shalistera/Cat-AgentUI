# Security

I'm the person behind Cat-AgentUI. I built it, I maintain it, and I take care of security reports and fixes.

Found something that looks like a security issue? Please email me at [totoria.helmold@gmail.com](mailto:totoria.helmold@gmail.com). Include what you found, how to reproduce it, and which version you're using.

Please keep the details out of public issues and pull requests until we've had a chance to look into it and work on a fix.

Thanks for helping keep Cat-AgentUI safe!
